Round 48: New calculator icons (beta), two-tone Edit / Copy / Paste / Share, and data-table fixes (as built)

New calculator icons (Settings › Calculator, beta): every function key on the Basic, Calculus, Statistics and Complex pages gets a two-tone picture of what it does, ink for what it works on and the accent for what it makes: π as a circle and its half-turn, e as its growing curve, ln and log as the log curve, ∫ as the shaded area, lim as a curve running into its asymptote, the statistics keys as their distributions, the complex keys on the plane. Trigonometry, matrices, letters and constants keep their labels; holding a key still explains it (names are under the keys here only for review). Edit, Copy, Paste and Share are now the app's own two-tone icons everywhere (menus, history, steps, the converter, exports, the table). In the table: a bolder bold, closer exponent digits, Alignment standing as an upright connected group on tablets, the zoom buttons' + and − showing again, the + button clear of them, and no arrow keys.
