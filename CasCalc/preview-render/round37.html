Round 37: Room to type on phones

Adding a line, note or folder, editing a line, or tapping the keyboard button hides the graph and its buttons (＋, import, export, tools). The list takes the room above the keyboard and shows every line, scrolling, not just the one being edited, with the cursor kept in view. Enter, or pulling the keyboard's handle down, brings the graph back. The same applies in 2D, 3D and the complex plane; construct mode closes when this starts.
