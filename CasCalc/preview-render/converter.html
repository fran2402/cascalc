Unit converter BETA · history chip

Turned on in Settings › Calculator (beta), then in the ⋮ menu. Units are typed as expressions (km/s/Mpc, W/(m² sr Hz), cm-3, ²·³) or picked from the catalog; SI, SI base and CGS write the answer in that system. When the dimensions differ, c, h (or ħ) and k_B bridge them. Every value below is the engine's real output.
