Sums in closed form

Infinite sums come out as ζ, η, ln, π, eˣ, sin… from the shape of the term; with a letter in the exponent the answer is a function (η(z)), which plots on the real line and the complex plane. Long exact numbers show their decimal first (≈), one tap from the exact form.

Number settings

Settings › Numbers: exact or decimal answers first, when long exact numbers switch to decimals, the format, and (in Auto) where scientific notation starts.

Complex plane: taps, areas, colors

Points and curves take the 2D graph's colors. Tapping a curve shows t, z and f(z) with the areas it offers: inside (closed curves, polygons, loops), to the real axis (tap again for the end), and between curves where another crosses. Results use the compact card on phones; typed ∮ lines show their value on it too. t is among the quick letters.
