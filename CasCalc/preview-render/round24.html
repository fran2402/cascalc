Spreadsheet formulas in the data table (beta)

With "Spreadsheet formulas" on under Settings › Calculator (Beta), the data table works like a spreadsheet. Columns are lettered A, B, C… on their cards. A cell starting with = is a formula: =B1/A1, =SUM(A:A), =SLOPE(B:B, A:A), =IF(A1>0, A1, 0). It shows its value, with a small ƒx, until you tap it to edit, and errors (#DIV/0!, #REF!, #NAME?, #CYCLE!…) are edged in red. Formula cells can be x, y or σ like any other, so a computed column can be plotted and fitted. A column's ⋮ menu gains "Fill the formula down", which copies the first formula to every row below. Relative references move with it and $-fixed ones stay, as in Excel. The ƒx button in the toolbar lists about 60 functions (sums and statistics, conditional COUNTIF/SUMIF, regression, logic, rounding, logs and trig). Picking one starts it in the cell you last typed in.
