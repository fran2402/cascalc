History, redesigned

The answer leads, large and right-aligned, with = or ≈ in the accent color; the question sits above, small and muted. When there's a decimal as well as the exact form, the other form is under the answer: tap ⇅ to swap. The newest card (or any card you tap) is raised and shows its actions: Graph, Use, Copy, Share (image, LaTeX, or delete). Tapping the math still reuses it, holding copies it (with a "copied" toast), swiping deletes; cards slide into place.
