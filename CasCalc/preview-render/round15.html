Integral results

One card for every integral picked on a graph: an icon and title, the integral as it's written, the value large, smaller figures in tiles, and Use (into the calculator) or Copy. Top left of the plot, under the legend; × closes it.

Complex plane: point card, points and curves

Tap the plane: the same card as the 2D graph, with z and f(z), each with its polar form and a Use button (the complex number goes into the calculator). New lines: a number is a point (2 + i), a list is points ([1 + i, −1, −i], joinable into a polygon), and an expression in t is a curve z(t) (eit, with a range after a comma: 0 ≤ t ≤ π). The AC key becomes [ ] as in 2D, and f(z) = … is plotted.
