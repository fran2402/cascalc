(nk)
Binomial coefficient
Statistics tab · long-pressed key
(nk)=n!k! (n − k)!
What it is

The number of ways to choose k things from n, when order doesn’t matter. Also the coefficients of (a + b)n.

How to use
  1. 1Tap the key: a stack of two boxes appears.
  2. 2Type n in the top box, then → to move down.
  3. 3Type k in the bottom box, then =.
Examples
(52)=10
Pairs from 5 people.
↵ Try it
(n2)=n(n − 1)2
Letters work too: kept exact.

Legendre polynomial
Polynomials tab · Pn(x)
(n + 1) Pn + 1(x) = (2n + 1) x Pn(x) − n Pn − 1(x)
P0 = 1,   P1 = x
What it is

Polynomials orthogonal on [−1, 1]: ∫−11 PmPn dx = 0 for m ≠ n. They describe gravity and charge around a sphere.

How to use
  1. 1Tap the key: it shows P□(□).
  2. 2Type the degree n in the small box, then →.
  3. 3Type the variable or a number in the brackets, then =.
  4. 4Graph it as y = P3(x), or long-press = for the steps.
