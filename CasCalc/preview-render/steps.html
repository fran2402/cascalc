Steps BETA

Settings › Calculator › Show steps (marked Beta, off by default). Then an integral or ∮ in the history gets a Steps button; it opens the working: a vertical stepper where each step is a numbered node on a line, with the rule, one sentence and the math, and lettered smaller steps inside. Next step reveals one at a time (or Show all); the check by differentiation and the answer close it. Phone: a full-height sheet. Tablet: a two-pane dialog, question, answer and an outline on the left. Every step comes from the method the integrator actually used.

Tablet: question, answer and outline on the left (tap a step to jump to it), the stepper on the right.
