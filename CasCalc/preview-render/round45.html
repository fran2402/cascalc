Round 45: Many more Excel and Sheets commands in the ribbon (as built)

Every tab grew. Home: Paste formats and Paste transposed, Format Painter, a new Font group (Bold, Italic, Fill color ▾, Change case ▾), Currency ▾ ($ € £ ¥), Find & Select ▾ (Find and replace, Go to…, Select all / column / row), and AutoSum ▾ now also has MEDIAN, PRODUCT and STDEV. Insert: Random ▾ (whole, 0 to 1, normal), Series, 1, 2, 3…, Today, Now and =TODAY(). Formulas: Distributions and Engineering categories, Fill formula down, Formulas to values. Data: Randomize and Reverse rows, Fill blanks, To numbers, Unique, and New column ▾ (running total, difference, % of total, rank, z-score, scaled 0 to 1). View: Hide column and Unhide, Go to, Select all. 19 new custom icons. On a phone all of these appear in the bottom bar and the ⌃ list.
