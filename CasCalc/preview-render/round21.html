Data table, redesigned

Each column is now a card tinted with its role. The role shows as a badge (x and y round, σ's rounded squares), next to the column's name. The card's ⋮ menu has the roles, plus sort rows by this column, fill 1, 2, 3…, clear and remove. Cells are tinted by their column's role and sit in banded rows. The cell being typed in gets a primary outline, cells that aren't numbers get a red edge, and Next on the keyboard moves on. Tap a row number to insert a row above or below, or remove it. A floating toolbar holds paste (CSV or tab-separated, e.g. from a spreadsheet), add a column, remove empty rows and preview, with a large "+ Row" button beside it. On a tablet a side pane shows the counts, a live preview of the points with their error bars, and what each role means.

Steps: all at once, and nested

Every step shows straight away, so there's no Next step / Show all bar. An operation inside another is now worked out from the inside: here the ∮ for a symbolic radius R (for R larger than every singularity's distance, both poles are inside), its residues, the residue theorem, then the limit. |z| written with bars in LaTeX is now an absolute value, as on the keys.

Slider, converter and picker

The ℝ/ℤ toggle is back as a button beside the slider's name. The converter's swap button showed in the mockup but not in the app: its 0 dp-tall row squeezed it to nothing, and it now overflows the row as designed. In the picker, "none" is the first prefix, tapping the chosen prefix again turns it off, and × writes "·", which ends a denominator: lea/Å·in/Å is (lea/Å)(in/Å). A plain space still keeps the J/kg K convention.
