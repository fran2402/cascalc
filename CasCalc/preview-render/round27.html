Documentation, spreadsheet editing, symbols and exact conversions

Settings › Documentation opens a manual: a contents page with search, then chapters numbered by part (The calculator, Graphs and tools, Reference). Each chapter has "On this page" links, numbered sections, notes, formulas, and tables of worked examples. There are 160 examples, and each answer was computed by the calculator itself (a test keeps them in step). The Reference part lists every key and the spreadsheet functions. In the data table, a formula being typed outlines each range it uses in its own color, matching functions appear above the keyboard, and the corner square fills down or across like Excel. Built symbols such as x̂₁ stay whole when sent to a graph. Unit conversions use exact decimals.
