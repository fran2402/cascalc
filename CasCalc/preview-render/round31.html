Round 31: GeoGebra-style geometry in the 2D graph (alpha)

With Settings › Graphs › Geometry on, a line can be a construction: A = (1, 2) is a draggable point, and GeoGebra's commands (Segment, Circle, Polygon, Midpoint, Intersect, bisectors, Tangent, Angle, Area, Reflect, Rotate…) build on it. Points carry their names on the graph, angles their arcs and values, and numbers show their value under the line. Construct (top right) gives tap tools, like GeoGebra's toolbar, and ＋ › Geometry lists every command.
