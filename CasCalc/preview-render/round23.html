History, organized

Full-screen history is now an organized list, newest first, instead of a chat-style feed. Pinned calculations come first, then the rest by when they were done: Today, Yesterday, This week, This month, then each month. Each section has a sticky header with its count, and its rows form one rounded group, with large corners at the ends and small ones between. Each row has a badge for its kind (∫, d/dx, lim, Σ, matrix, equation, number), the question small and muted, the answer large, and its pin and folder. Tap a row to open it into the full card with its actions (Steps, Graph, Use, Copy, Pin, Folder, Share). Swipe to delete. Using a calculation goes back to the calculator with it typed in. The search, the All / Pinned / folder chips and Export sit above. Calculations get a time from now on; older ones are listed under "Earlier".
