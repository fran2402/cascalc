Round 39: Geometry in space and on the complex plane

Geometry mode now builds in the 3D graph and on the complex plane too. In space, points have three coordinates and there are planes, spheres and solids: planes are cut to the box and drawn see-through with spheres and solids, sorted by depth with the surfaces. On the complex plane, points are numbers (A = 1 + 2i), drawn over the colouring with a dark outline; products, roots of unity and the image of a shape under f(z) are commands and tools. Construct works the same way in all three graphs, offering the tools that fit each one.
