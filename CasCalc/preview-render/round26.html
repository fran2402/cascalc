History, data table, fit statistics, How to use, converter

History cards now have one row of actions: a main button (Graph, or Use), Steps when available, Copy and Pin as icons, and ⋮ for the folder, sharing and delete. The full history shows every calculation in full instead of collapsed rows. The data table has a single + at the bottom left that opens to Row, Column and Paste. Rows and columns resize with the grips (double-tap a grip to reset), and cells stay plain text until tapped, which keeps long tables smooth. Fitting left the table: holding Fit in the graph shows the statistics, including χ²/ν, with the curve and residuals drawn at the data's point size (now 1 to 16). Settings has a How to use section, which is a sub-page on a phone. The converter shows 3 × 10 with the exponent raised and true minus signs.
