Round 28

Advanced examples in the mode guides are now a solid tertiary card with a matching Try button, which reads properly in the dark theme. The data table gains undo and redo in its top bar, its + moves to the right, and double-tapping the fill handle fills down to the end of the data. History cards put Copy and Pin as small icons beside ⋮. Settings › Graphs lists 2D field quality, 3D surface detail, then complex plot quality (Low, Medium, High), all Low by default. A new Home screen section pins shortcuts to the unit converter and each graph mode; they're also on the app icon. A graph file opened from another app is plotted in its mode, asking first if that graph already has lines.
