Mode guides · hold a mode button

Holding Calculator, 2D, 3D or Complex opens that mode's guide: a hero with what the mode is for, example cards you can tap to type straight into that mode (the guide closes and the mode opens), then gestures and tips. On phones it's a draggable bottom sheet with chips to jump between the four guides; on tablets a wide two-pane dialog with the modes in a rail.

Tablet: the modes in a rail on the left, three columns of examples, gestures and tips side by side.
