Round 17 · vector fields on the 2D graph

Type a pair with x or y in it: (−y, x), F(x, y) = (sin y, sin x), a column vector, or ∇f. Conditions after a comma (x² + y² < 9) limit where arrows are drawn; other letters get sliders. Long-press the dot: arrow color (this color, or by length along any colormap, the complex plane's picker), length (scaled, same length, true length), scale, arrowhead (triangle, stealth, open, dot, none) and its size, how many arrows, thickness. Tap the field for F and |F| there.
