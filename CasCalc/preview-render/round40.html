Round 40: Spreadsheet tools in the data table

The data table gains the everyday tools of Sheets and Excel: find and replace, filters by condition, a selected range with its sum, average and count, number formats (fixed decimals, percent, scientific, thousands grouped), a red–yellow–green color scale, a frozen first column, and tidying (duplicate rows, spaces, rows ↔ columns). The fill handle now counts on from text (Week 1 → Week 2, Jan → Feb). On phones the title is just "Data".
