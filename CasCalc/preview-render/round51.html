Round 51: Roomier words, optically aligned letters, two-tone tab and plot icons, and more of the app's own icons (as built)

Words (trigonometry, the matrix keys, ln, mod, n!, Ans, Re, Im, Res) are built from one set of letters on a shared baseline and x-height, round letters (s, o, e, a) dipping just past both so they look level, more space between letters, and each word on a canvas sized to it; Ans is one color. The keypad tabs, the coordinate toggles (Cartesian, cylindrical, spherical, polar grid), the complex plot options (modulus bands, phase lines, the Re/Im grid), the loop integral and geometry are now two-tone. New app icons: search, history, undo, redo, show and hide, info, tips, keyboard, colors, play, pause, replay and length.
