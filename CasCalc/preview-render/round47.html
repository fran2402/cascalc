Round 47: Two-tone icons, a Material 3 Expressive ribbon, and the typewriter font in the cells (as built)

Every data-table icon now has two tones, as the geometry tools do: what a command acts on in the ink color, and what it makes or does in the accent color (the arrow of a sort, the plus of an insert, the Σ of AutoSum, the dots of Random). The ribbon is Material 3 Expressive: tabs with a sliding pill, each group in its own rounded card, large buttons as tonal shapes that square up when pressed or on, small ones as pills, Bold | Italic and Left | Center | Right as connected button groups, menus' ▾ turning over when open, and a round tonal fold button. Cells are in Computer Modern's typewriter (New Computer Modern Mono, the Unicode CMU Typewriter), with its true italic, not slanted roman.
