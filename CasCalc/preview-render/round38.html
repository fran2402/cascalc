Round 38: New data table features

A column's ⋮ now has Statistics, Sort largest first, Fill with a series, Duplicate and Move left/right, as well as the roles and the existing options. A row's number can duplicate the row. A new ⋮ at the top shares or copies the whole table as CSV (formulas give their values) and removes empty rows. Spreadsheet formulas are out of beta, so the chip just says Formulas. Everything can be undone.
