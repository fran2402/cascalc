Round 46: Bold, italic and fill color per cell, and pinch to zoom (as built)

Bold, Italic and Fill color now style the selected cell or range, not the whole column, as in Excel and Sheets. Each cell keeps its look when rows are sorted, shuffled, inserted or deleted, when columns move, and when it is cut, copied, pasted or painted with Format Painter; Paste values leaves looks behind, and Clear › formats removes them. Two fingers pinch the table to zoom between 60% and 160%, while one finger still scrolls and taps.
