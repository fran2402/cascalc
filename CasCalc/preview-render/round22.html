Curve fitting in the data table

The table's toolbar has a new Fit button. Choose a line, quadratic, cubic, exponential, power, logarithm, or a formula of your own (every letter other than x is fitted). With a σ(y) column the fit is weighted. Each parameter comes with its standard error, plus R², RMSE, χ²/ν (when weighted) and the degrees of freedom. The curve is drawn over the points with their error bars, the residuals go below, and "Add to graph" adds the fitted line. Every number here is the engine's output for the table's sample data.

Tangent, normal and arc length · slope fields

A point on a function now offers Tangent and Normal, which add that line to the graph, and Arc length, which measures to the next tap, like the area tools. A line like y′ = x − y (or dy/dx = …) draws a slope field. Tap anywhere to draw the solution through that point (RK4, forwards and backwards). Tap a solution to remove it or clear them all. They're saved with the graph, and work on log axes and in exports.

History: search, pins, folders, export

In full-screen history there's now a search pill (it matches questions and answers as typed), chips for All, Pinned and each folder (hold a folder to rename or empty it), and an Export button. A card's actions add Pin and Folder. Pinned calculations and those in folders are kept whatever the history limit, and Clear history leaves them. Export writes what's listed as a PDF (A4, typeset in Computer Modern, numbered, answers on the right) or a LaTeX document.
