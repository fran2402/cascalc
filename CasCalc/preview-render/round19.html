Graph sliders · real or integer

The ℝ/ℤ button moved out of the slider row, which was crowded, into a row of its own under the slider. The range's ends sit at each side, and a small two-part switch in the middle reads "ℝ real | ℤ integer". The chosen part fills and rounds off. Tap an end to edit the range.

Unit converter, redesigned BETA

Each unit is now one big button showing the unit in LaTeX and what it measures, with a ▾. Tap it to open the picker. The answer comes first in the To card, in large type. Under it is one row of quick targets: SI, SI base and CGS, then units the From unit converts to. Typing a unit moved into the picker. Significant figures and the c/h/k_B equivalences are grouped under Options. All numbers are the engine's real output.

Unit symbols in New Computer Modern

Computer Modern has no Å, é, ø, ō, ‰ or ☉, so these units used to fall back to Roboto in the middle of the math. They now come from New Computer Modern: an upright subset (Latin-1 and Latin Extended-A, ‰, ℃, ℉, Å, ☉, ☽) and an italic Latin subset, which slot in after Computer Modern and before Roboto.
