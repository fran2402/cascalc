Round 41: The data table, redesigned after Sheets and Excel (Material 3 Expressive)

A real spreadsheet grid (lettered column headers, numbered rows, thin gridlines, a selected cell with a fill handle) instead of separate cards. Above it, a formula bar shows and edits the selected cell, as in Sheets and Excel, with its address in a pill. A toolbar in tabs (Home, Insert, Data, Format) gathers what was spread over menus: an expressive connected button group for the tabs and big tonal buttons. A column's header opens a bottom sheet with its role as a segmented button and its actions as tiles. A floating pill sums the selection; the expressive FAB adds rows, columns or a paste. On a tablet the toolbar becomes a ribbon of labelled groups, and a side panel shows the points live and the selected column's role, format, filter and statistics.
