**CAS Calculator** is designed and built to function as a standalone, offline-capable utility tool.

* **Zero Data Collection:** We do not access, collect, record, store, transmit, process, or share any personal, sensitive, or technical user data.

* **No Device Trackers:** We do not track your activity across other apps or websites, nor do we collect persistent device identifiers (such as IMEI, IMSI, SIM Serial Number, or Android Advertising ID).
